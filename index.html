About this data. These are the official AITA ranking lists, covering every published category — Boys and Girls U-12, U-14, U-16 and U-18, plus Men's and Women's Singles and Doubles. Pick a category from the dropdown to search within it.

This page updates automatically. A scheduled check runs regularly and republishes fresh data whenever AITA's rankings change — nothing for you to do.

Choose your colours: the buttons at the top switch the whole site between four court themes — Hard Court, Clay Court, Wimbledon and Neon Night. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Three ways to search: a full or partial name (pick from the dropdown), an AITA registration number, or just a rank number (like "42") to see who currently holds that spot.

About the Compare tab's commentary: the "matchup preview" is generated from each player's current rank and points gap only. AITA doesn't publish match history or head-to-head records, so this reads today's standing, not a prediction backed by real results.
